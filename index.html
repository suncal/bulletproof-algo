Do I need to be a programmer?

You need to be able to run a Python script and use a terminal. If you've ever installed a Python package with pip, you're qualified. If you've never written code, this isn't for you — try a "no-code" tool first.

Will this make me money?

Maybe. Maybe not. The strategy has a 72% backtest win rate over 6 years; my live paper performance has been mixed (we just finished a +$248 week and a flat week). Real-world performance is typically 60–75% of backtest. Anyone promising guaranteed returns is lying. I'm teaching you to build the engine — what you do with it is up to you.

Do I need a $25K account?

For real money, yes — under $25K you hit PDT limits that skip most signals and cripple returns. For learning, no — Alpaca paper accounts are free and unlimited. Build it on paper first. Move to real money only when you've seen 60+ days of live behavior.

Why $497? Why not $97 or $1997?

No. I'm not an investment adviser. I'm a builder teaching other builders how to build. The course teaches engineering, not financial advice. The strategies are examples — you choose what to run, how to size it, when to stop.
